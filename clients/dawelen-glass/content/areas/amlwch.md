## Exposure is the whole story up here

There is nothing between this coast and the Irish Sea, so the glazing takes
the full force of it: salt, driven rain and sustained wind pressure on the
glass. Units on the weather side fail years before the same units on the
sheltered side of the house.

Because the whole elevation is exposed to the same conditions, they tend to
fail together — which looks alarming, and is still much cheaper to put right
than the replacement windows you will be quoted for elsewhere.

## After a gale

Storm damage is its own category of work: the property needs making safe and
weathertight first, and the proper glazing follows once the glass is in. If
the house is unoccupied or a holiday property, say so when you ring, because
that changes what we do first.
