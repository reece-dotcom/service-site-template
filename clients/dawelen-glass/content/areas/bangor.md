## The nearest city, and closer than it sounds

Bangor sits across the Menai Strait, which makes it feel like a different
area — but from Benllech it is a shorter run than Rhosneigr or Holyhead. If
you are in the city and have been told a glazier will have to come from
Caernarfon or Llandudno, we are worth a call first.

## A lot of rented housing

The private rented sector here is large, and older terraced housing that has
been let for decades throws up a particular set of jobs: door glass that
predates current safety standards, sealed units retro-fitted into frames that
were never designed for them, and panes broken between tenancies.

We are happy to work through landlords and agents, invoice properly, and
report back in writing on what we found — which matters when nobody who rings
us has actually seen the window.

## Older frames, sensible answers

Much of the Victorian and Edwardian stock in the city has frames worth
keeping. Where a sealed unit has failed in one of those, replacing the unit
and leaving the joinery alone is usually both the cheaper and the better
answer — and we will say so rather than quoting you for windows.
