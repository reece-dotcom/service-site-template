## A town where the frame usually has to stay

Beaumaris has a dense run of listed and protected frontages, and on those
buildings the window itself is part of what is protected. That makes glass
repair the practical route: the sealed unit, the pane or the obscure panel
gets changed and the frame, the sightline and the appearance of the elevation
stay as they are.

## Older glass, non-standard sizes

Georgian and Victorian openings are rarely square, and the glass in them is
often thinner than anything modern. We measure the rebate and order to that,
and where a slim unit is the only thing that will physically fit, that is what
we specify.

## Holiday accommodation

The town has a high proportion of guest houses, hotels and holiday lets,
where a broken pane or a misted unit is a booking problem with a date on it.
Tell us the changeover day and we will work to it.
