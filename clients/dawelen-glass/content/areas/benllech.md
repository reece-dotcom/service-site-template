## Why the seaward side always goes first

Properties along this stretch of coast take weather from the north east
across the bay, and that is what kills sealed units: wind-driven rain,
constant salt, and big temperature swings on the glass. The result is very
consistent — the front elevation mists up while the back of the house is
untouched.

That is worth knowing before anyone quotes you for a full house of new
windows. If the frames are sound, replacing the failed units on one
elevation is a much smaller job.

## Holiday lets

A lot of the housing stock here works as holiday accommodation, where a
broken pane is an urgent problem tied to a changeover date rather than a
general inconvenience. Tell us the dates and we will work to them where we
can.
