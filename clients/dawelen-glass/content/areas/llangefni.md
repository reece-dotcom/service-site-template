## Inland, so a different pattern of failure

Away from the coast the units last longer, and the work here leans towards
breakage, cracked panes and units that have simply reached the end of a
twenty-year life. Where a coastal job is often one elevation gone at once,
an inland job is usually one or two windows.

## Commercial as well as domestic

Being the island's main town, there is a run of shops, offices and public
buildings here. Commercial glass has its own requirements — manifestation on
large panes so people can see them, safety glass in doors — and it almost
always needs making safe the same day rather than the same week.
