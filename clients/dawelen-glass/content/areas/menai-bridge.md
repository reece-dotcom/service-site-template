## Old frames, awkward measurements

On stone cottages and Victorian terraces the opening is rarely square and
the frame has usually moved. Glass cut to the size the window was meant to be
does not go in. We measure the rebate as it is now, on site, and order to
that.

## Conservation and appearance

Parts of the town are visually sensitive, and on a front elevation the
pattern and finish of the glass is part of how the building looks. Matching
what is there matters more here than it does on a modern estate.

## Rented property

Between the town and Bangor across the Strait there is a lot of rented
housing, where glazing repairs sit with the landlord and need doing to a
deadline. We are happy to invoice the landlord or agent directly.
