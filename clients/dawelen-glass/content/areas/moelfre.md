## The sea decides the lifespan

Moelfre and the stretch towards Traeth Lligwy and Dulas face north east into
open water. The properties on the seaward side of the village get the full
run of salt air and onshore wind, and their sealed units fail years earlier
than identical units a few streets inland.

If you are replacing glass on a property here, it is worth specifying
properly rather than fitting the cheapest unit available — the last one
failed early for a reason that has not gone away.

## Second homes and lets

A good share of the housing along this coast is holiday accommodation,
often empty when the damage happens and discovered days later. Tell us if the
property is unoccupied and whether anyone is local to give access, because
that changes how we plan the visit.

## A short run from Benllech

Moelfre is up the coast from our base, on the same road. It is one of the
easier places on the island for us to reach, including for a quick look at a
broken pane.
