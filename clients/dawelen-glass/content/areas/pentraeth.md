## Village housing and a lot of it inland

Pentraeth sits back from the coast, which means the glazing lasts longer here
than it does on the seafront at Benllech or Red Wharf Bay. The failures we
see are age rather than exposure: units fitted in the eighties and nineties
reaching the end of their life, and the odd pane broken by a stone, a ball or
a branch.

## Farms, outbuildings and sheds

There are working farms and smallholdings all round the village, where the
glass that needs sorting is as likely to be a workshop window or an
outbuilding pane as a living-room unit. Those are straightforward jobs and
we would rather do them on the same visit than make two trips.

## Close to base

The village is a few minutes from Benllech, so measuring visits here rarely
need much notice. If you are between Pentraeth and the coast, ring and ask —
you are almost certainly inside the area we cover daily.
