## An exposed west-coast village

Rhosneigr faces the open sea on the west side of the island, and the glazing
shows it. Sand, salt and sustained onshore wind are hard on perimeter seals
and on any coating that is already compromised, so misting here tends to
arrive on the weather side of a property first and spread along that
elevation.

## Second homes, let out and lived in

A large share of the housing is holiday accommodation and second homes, which
changes the practical side of the work more than the glazing side: access,
who holds the keys, and what happens when the damage is found on a
changeover rather than the day it happened.

## The far side of the island

Rhosneigr is a proper drive from Benllech, so we group west-coast work
together with Holyhead and Valley. That means a measuring visit here is worth
booking in rather than left until something is broken.
