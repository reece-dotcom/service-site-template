A broken pane stops being a glazing problem and becomes a security problem.
Here is the order to deal with it in, and what to expect from us when you
ring.

## 1. Make the area safe before you make it tidy

Get people and pets out of the room, put shoes on, and deal with the large
pieces first. Broken glass travels much further than you would expect —
check the far side of the room and outside beneath the window.

If it is a toughened pane that has shattered into thousands of small cubes,
do not brush it towards a doorway. Get it into a rigid container rather than
a bin bag.

## 2. Photograph it before you touch anything else

Two or three photos of the window, the room and any damage. If you are
claiming on insurance, or the landlord is, this is the evidence — and it
takes ten seconds now versus an argument later.

## 3. If a crime may be involved, report it first

For a break-in or criminal damage, get a crime reference number before the
window is repaired. Insurers ask for it, and once the glass is out there is
nothing left to look at.

## 4. Expect boarding up tonight, glass later

This is the part that surprises people: replacement glass is cut to measure.
Nobody carries the size of your window on a van. So the first visit makes the
opening secure and weathertight — boarded up properly, fixed to the frame
rather than propped — and the glazing happens once the glass arrives.

That is not a stalling tactic, it is the only way to do it properly. A
property that is secure tonight and glazed correctly next week is a better
outcome than the wrong glass fitted in a hurry.

## 5. Tell us these four things when you ring

- Roughly where you are on the island.
- Whether the property is currently open to the outside.
- Whether it is a window, a door, or glass next to a door.
- Whether the house is occupied, empty, or a let.

That last pair matters more than it sounds: door glass and low-level panes
are critical locations under the building regulations and have to be
toughened or laminated, so knowing what the opening is tells us what glass to
order. And an empty holiday property with an open window is a different
priority to one where someone is home.

## What we will not do

We will not fit ordinary float glass into a door because it is what came out,
and we will not quote you for a new window when the frame is sound and the
pane is the problem.

If the frame has been damaged as well as the glass — forced, split or
distorted — we will say so, because in that case glass alone is not the
repair.
