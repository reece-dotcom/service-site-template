## The awkward measurements

Almost none of this work comes in standard sizes. Alcoves taper, chimney
breasts are out of square, and older cottage walls are rarely flat in either
direction. So the job starts with templating the space rather than taking one
width and one height.

## Where toughened glass is not optional

Behind a hob, in a shower, and in anything a person could walk into, glass
has to be toughened. Toughened glass is made to final size and then heat
treated — it cannot be trimmed, drilled or notched afterwards. Every cut-out
and every hole is decided before it goes into the furnace, which is why we
measure carefully and then confirm the drawing with you before ordering.
