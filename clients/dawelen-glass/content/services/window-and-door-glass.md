## Measured, then cut

Glass is made to the measurement, so the measurement has to be right. We
measure the sight size and the rebate on site rather than working from what
the old unit was supposed to be, because frames move, particularly older
timber ones.

## Specification matters more than people expect

Three things change what goes into the opening:

**Safety glass.** Doors, the panels beside them, and anything glazed below
800mm need to meet impact-safety requirements. That means toughened or
laminated, not ordinary float glass.

**Privacy.** Bathrooms and anything overlooked take obscure glass, and the
pattern wants to match whatever else is in the house.

**Exposure.** On a seaward elevation the argument for a better coating and a
properly specified cavity is a lot stronger than it is on a sheltered
terrace inland.

## Conservatories

Conservatory glazing fails in its own way: the roof panels pick up algae and
the side panels mist like any other sealed unit. Replacing the failed panels
is generally a fraction of the cost of a new roof, which is what most people
have been quoted for by the time they ring.
