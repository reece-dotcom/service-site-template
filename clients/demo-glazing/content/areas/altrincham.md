## Altrincham and the surrounding villages

The housing stock changes fast around here — Victorian villas in Bowdon,
1930s semis through Timperley, new-build estates out towards Broadheath. The
right window for one is the wrong window for another.

## Conservation areas

Hale, Bowdon and parts of central Altrincham have conservation status. That
restricts what you can fit on anything visible from the street. It's not a
reason to avoid replacement — it's a reason to have the planning conversation
before the frames are ordered rather than after.
