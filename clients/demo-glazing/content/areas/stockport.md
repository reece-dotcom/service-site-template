## Working in Stockport

Being local isn't a marketing line here — it changes the job. If a handle
needs adjusting six months after fitting, we're twenty minutes away rather
than sending someone from another county in a fortnight.

## What we see most around here

The Victorian and Edwardian terraces around the town centre almost always
need sash or flush casement styles to look right; a standard casement on a
period front elevation is the fastest way to make a house look worse than
before you spent the money.

Out towards Davenport and Heaton Moor, the 1930s semis usually have bay
windows that need proper structural support during replacement. That's a
survey question, not a fitting-day surprise.
