If your conservatory is a storage room for nine months of the year, the
problem is almost certainly the roof.

## Why polycarbonate fails you

Polycarbonate has effectively no insulation value. In July it turns the
room into a greenhouse; in January the heat leaves faster than a radiator
can replace it. Blinds help with glare and do nothing for temperature.

## What a warm roof conversion involves

The existing glazing bars come off and an insulated solid roof structure
goes on, usually finished with plasterboard inside and tiles or slate
outside. The room becomes part of the house rather than a bolt-on. Most
conversions take three to five days.

## The check that decides it

The existing frames and base have to carry the extra weight. Some
lightweight early-2000s builds can't, and no honest installer will tell you
otherwise after a two-minute look. We survey the base and the frames before
quoting, and occasionally we have to say a rebuild is the real answer.

## Building regulations

A solid roof conversion is notifiable — it's a structural change. It needs
sign-off. If someone offers to do it without, that's a problem you inherit
at resale.
