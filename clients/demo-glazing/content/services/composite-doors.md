## Why the core matters

The phrase "composite door" covers two very different products. A solid-timber
core door is heavy, rigid and stays put. A foam-filled one is lighter and
cheaper and, on an exposed elevation, will flex enough over time that the lock
stops engaging cleanly.

We only fit solid core. It costs more up front and it's the reason we don't
get callbacks in year three.

## The bits people forget

Everyone chooses a colour. Almost nobody asks about the threshold, and that's
what causes draughts. On a level-access threshold you need the right weather
seal, or you've bought a beautiful door that whistles every February.

## Security

A door is only as strong as its cylinder. We fit three-star cylinders as
standard rather than as an upsell, because the alternative is fitting a
£1,200 door with a £6 lock in it.
