## The honest version

Most conservatories in the North West get used for about five months a year.
The reason is nearly always the roof: polycarbonate, no insulation, and a
temperature swing that makes the room unusable at both ends of the year.

If you already have a conservatory that you don't use, the conversation to have
isn't about a new one. It's about whether the existing frames and base can
carry a warm roof — which is a much smaller job and, in a lot of cases, gets
you a room you'll actually use.

## Building it properly

We dig and inspect foundations rather than assuming what's under the slab.
On older properties around Stockport, what's under an existing patio is
routinely not what you'd hope for.
