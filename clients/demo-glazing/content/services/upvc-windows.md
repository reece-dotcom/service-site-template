## What we fit

Most Stockport homes end up in one of three camps: 1930s semis with original
timber casements, 1970s–80s estates on their first replacement windows, or
terraces where a previous owner had cheap frames put in around 2005 that have
since blown.

We survey each opening properly rather than working from a standard size list.
On older properties that usually means dealing with a bit of rot in the reveal
or a lintel that has dropped — better found at survey than on fitting day.

## Frame styles

**Casement** — the standard side or top hung window. Sensible, secure, and
the cheapest per opening.

**Flush casement** — sits level with the frame, closer to the look of the
original timber. Worth the extra on a period front elevation.

**Sliding sash** — for terraces and Victorian properties where a casement
would look wrong. More expensive, but on the right house it's the only option
that doesn't look like a compromise.
