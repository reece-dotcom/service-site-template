## Terraces and knock-throughs

A lot of Barking's older terraces have had the two ground-floor rooms
joined. A pair of internal glass doors in that opening gives the front room back its
own space without losing the light from the garden end of the house.

## New-build glazing

Barking Riverside and the Abbey Road developments are only a few years old,
but misted and cracked units still turn up. We can replace the sealed unit
in most systems once the developer warranty has lapsed or refused.
