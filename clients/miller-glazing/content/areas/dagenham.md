## Becontree houses and steel-look doors

The typical Becontree house has a narrow hall running past a front room to
a kitchen at the back, and the kitchen is where families actually live. A
glazed steel-look door between hall and kitchen — or a pair where the wall
has been opened — brings the light from the back of the house into the
hall without turning it into one open room. The slim black frame suits the
1930s proportions better than the pine-and-glass doors most of these houses
were given in the eighties.

## Repairs before replacements

Dagenham gets more than its share of replacement-window sales calls. Before
you agree to a full set, have the units looked at: if the frames are sound,
new sealed units cost a fraction and keep the windows you already have.
