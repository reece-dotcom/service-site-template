## Extension openings

The classic Hornchurch alteration is a rear kitchen extension leaving a
wide opening into the original dining room. Internal bifold doors are the
usual answer: a four-leaf set folds flat
to one side and gives you both rooms back; a slider suits a narrower room
where the stack would get in the way. See
[internal bifold and sliding doors](/services/internal-bifold-and-sliding-doors/).

## Emerson Park

Larger houses, larger screens. Full-width glazed steel-look screens with a
door in them are the job here, and we set the bars out to line through with
the windows beyond.
