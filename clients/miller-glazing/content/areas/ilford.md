## Extensions and the room behind them

Rear extensions are everywhere in Ilford, and they leave an opening between
the original back room and the new kitchen. A glazed bifold there lets the
old room keep its identity while the light from the garden reaches it. See
[internal bifold and sliding doors](/services/internal-bifold-and-sliding-doors/).

## 1930s semis

The Gants Hill and Clayhall semis have wide halls and glazed front doors
that already let the light in; a steel-look door to the kitchen finishes
the job and looks as if it was always meant to be there.
