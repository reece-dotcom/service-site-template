## Old village, new estates

Rainham has two housing stocks a few streets apart: the cottages and semis
around the Village, and the new-build estates going up along the A1306.
The first bring us misted units and broken panes; the second bring us
cracked glass and, increasingly, owners who want a glazed steel-look screen
to break up an open-plan ground floor without losing the light.
