## Gidea Park and Emerson Park

Bigger houses, wider openings, higher ceilings. The steel-look French pair
is the door of choice here, often with a fixed glazed panel either side to
fill an opening that was widened years ago. Where the house still has its
original metal windows, we set the bars out to the same spacing.

## Collier Row, Harold Wood and the semis

The 1930s and post-war semis around the edges of Romford have had their
kitchens opened up like everywhere else in the borough, and the request is
nearly always a bifold or slider between the kitchen and the room behind.
Misted units in the same houses are a standard job for us.
