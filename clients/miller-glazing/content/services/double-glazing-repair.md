## What has failed

A double glazed unit is two panes with a spacer bar and a sealed cavity.
When the seal around the edge gives up, the cavity is no longer sealed:
moisture gets in, and on a cold morning it condenses on the inside faces of
the glass where nothing can reach it.

The frame is almost never the problem. On the 1930s and post-war housing
across Dagenham and Barking most of the uPVC windows went in twenty or
thirty years ago, and it is the units that are reaching the end of their
life, not the frames.

## What we do

Measure the unit — width, height, thickness and spacer — order glass to
that specification, then swap it. Beads off, old unit out, new unit in,
beads back. On a standard casement that is well under an hour once the
glass is in.

For a crack or a break we do the same, but we will board the opening first
if the replacement has to be made, so the property is secure in the
meantime. For glass in internal doors and screens, see
[steel-look internal doors](/services/steel-look-internal-doors/).

## What it costs

Per unit by size and specification, quoted after measuring. A plain unit is

number. We give you a fixed figure per unit rather than a house price that
