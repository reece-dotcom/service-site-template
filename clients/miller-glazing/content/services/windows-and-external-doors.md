## Windows

uPVC casements for most houses; aluminium where the owner wants slimmer
frames or a colour that will not fade. Openers, trickle vents, obscure
glass in bathrooms, and toughened glass where the opening is low enough to
need it.

Before we quote windows we look at the units. On a lot of the estates
around Dagenham and Romford the windows are fine and the sealed units have
gone — see [double glazing repair](/services/double-glazing-repair/) for
what that costs instead.

## External doors

- **French doors** — uPVC or aluminium, to a garden or a balcony.
- **Composite front doors** — a solid, insulated door in a range of colours
  and glazing panels.
- **External bifolds** — aluminium, to a patio or a garden room. If you
  have seen our [internal bifolds](/services/internal-bifold-and-sliding-doors/),
  these are the outdoor version with thermal breaks and weather seals.

## How we quote

One measuring visit, one fixed price in writing, valid for a sensible
period. It does not go up when you say yes and it does not come down if you
say you are thinking about it.
