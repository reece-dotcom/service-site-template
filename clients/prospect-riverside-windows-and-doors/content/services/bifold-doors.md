## The job Dave most wants to do

Bi-folds are the job that changes a house most for the money, and the one where a poor survey shows up fastest. Dave measures the structural opening himself, checks the floor levels inside and out, and specifies the threshold and drainage before anything is ordered.

## Configurations

A three-leaf set stacking to one side is the most common on a Chelmsford semi. Wider openings take four to seven leaves and can split to open from the middle. A traffic door, a single leaf that opens on its own, is worth having if you use the doors daily.

## What it costs

Priced per opening after the survey, in writing, with the threshold, glass spec and colour listed so you can compare like for like.
