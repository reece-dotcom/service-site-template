## Why composite

A timber door needs painting and a uPVC door can look thin. A composite door has a solid core with a tough skin, so it takes the weather without moving and never needs more than a wipe down. It is the door most of our customers in Chelmsford choose for the front of the house.

## Choosing yours

Style, colour inside and out, glass pattern, letterplate, handle and knocker are all picked at the survey from samples Dave brings with him. Side panels and matching back doors are made to the same spec so the house reads as one.

## The fitting day

The old door comes out in the morning, the new one is fitted, sealed and adjusted, and the team takes the old door and packaging away. You have a working, locked door by the afternoon.
