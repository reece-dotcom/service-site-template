## The two jobs we do most

The first is the warm-roof conversion: the polycarbonate or glass roof comes off, an insulated tiled roof goes on the existing frames, and the room becomes usable in every month. The second is a new build, from a simple lean-to to an Edwardian or P-shaped room, with the base, frames and roof all done by our team.

## What we check at the survey

Whether the existing base and frames will take a solid roof. Where the sun sits, which decides the glass. What the room is actually for, which decides the layout. Dave has surveyed enough of these to know which ones will disappoint, and he will say so.

## Pricing

A fixed written price for the whole job, including any Building Regulations work, so there is nothing to add on later.
