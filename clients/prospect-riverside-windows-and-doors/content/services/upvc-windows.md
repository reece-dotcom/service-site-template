## What a survey with us looks like

Dave comes out, measures every opening properly and talks through the styles that suit the house. You get a fixed price in writing within a couple of days. There is no second visit with a manager, and the price does not change when you say yes.

## Frames we fit

**Casement** is the everyday window and the best value per opening. **Flush casement** costs a little more and is worth it on the front of an older house. **Sliding sash** is for the terraces and period fronts where anything else looks wrong.

## After the fit

The FENSA certificate is registered within the month and posted to you, and the ten-year insurance-backed guarantee certificate comes with it. If a handle needs adjusting in a year, ring the same number and the same team comes back.
