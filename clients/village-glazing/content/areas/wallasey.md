## Working in Wallasey

Local means what it sounds like. If a handle needs adjusting a few weeks after
a repair, we're minutes away rather than sending someone across the water in a
fortnight.

## What we see most around here

Liscard and Poulton are full of 1990s uPVC on 1930s houses: sound frames,
worn-out hardware. That's the ideal repair job — the window is fine, the
mechanism isn't.

Nearer New Brighton and the promenade, the salt takes its toll. Friction
stays, handles and door gearboxes there fail years earlier than the same parts
would in an inland property, and they're all replaceable individually.
