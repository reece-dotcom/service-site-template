## Coastal wear

Wallasey, New Brighton and the seafront side of the Wirral are hard on window
hardware. Salt air eats friction stays and handle springs years before the
same window would fail inland, which is why hinges are one of the most common
call-outs we get around here.

## Lead lights

Older Wirral properties — and a lot of the 1930s semis — have leaded front
elevations that people are reluctant to lose. Repairing a damaged section
keeps the character of the house instead of swapping it for a plain unit that
never quite looks right.

## What it costs
