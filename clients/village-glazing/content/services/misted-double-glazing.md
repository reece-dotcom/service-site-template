## How we price it

Sealed units are priced on size and type, so we measure before quoting rather
than guessing over the phone. Toughened glass is a legal requirement in some
positions — low-level glazing, doors and side panels — and we spec that
correctly instead of putting in the cheapest pane that fits.

## The rest of the window matters too

While the glass is out we check the drainage slots and the beading. A unit
that sits in standing water because the drainage is blocked will blow again
in a few years, which is not a result anybody wants.

## Whole houses

If several windows have gone at once — common on estates where all the
glazing went in the same year — it's worth doing them in one visit. The
labour is cheaper per opening and the house looks consistent again.
