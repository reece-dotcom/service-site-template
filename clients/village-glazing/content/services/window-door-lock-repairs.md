## What actually breaks

On uPVC doors it is nearly always the multipoint mechanism — the long strip
down the edge of the door with the hooks and rollers. The gearbox inside it
does the work every time you lift the handle, and after fifteen years of
being forced against a door that has dropped a few millimetres, it gives up.

On windows it is usually the espagnolette or the handle itself: the cam wears,
the handle spins, or the little gearbox behind the handle stops driving the
locking points.

## What we do about it

We identify the mechanism (there are a lot of different systems on Wirral
housing stock), fit the correct replacement, then adjust the hinges and keeps
so the door closes and locks with a fingertip rather than a shoulder.

## Security while we're there

If your door still has an old euro cylinder, we'll say so. Anti-snap cylinders
to the current standard are inexpensive and they are the single most useful
security upgrade on a uPVC door.
